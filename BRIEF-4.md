# "One Terminal": the frog, in high definition (round 4)

Round 3 painted the studio and the devices (`refs/studio-d01.png`, `refs/studio-d09.png` are two of those plates).
The film puts our frog in that studio next to the devices, so he has to look like he belongs there: **a high-
definition, photoreal 3D character render**, lit exactly like the plates, not pixel art, not a flat cartoon.

## Who he is

Our own frog, the one in `refs/pixel/feels-stable.png` (the suit) and `refs/pixel/agent-pepe.png` (the face): green
skin with darker green shading, heavy-lidded smug half-closed eyes with ivory whites, wide brick-red lips, a calm,
knowing, slightly amused expression. In a **navy suit, white shirt, dark green tie**, a small square lapel pin with
no markings. Keep the face and proportions the same in every pose: it is one character, a premium 3D mascot in the
style of a modern app launch film (soft subsurface skin, fine fabric detail, crisp edges).

## Lighting and background (both matter)

- Light him like the plates: a dark ink studio, a cool soft ivory rim light from below and behind (the horizon
  glow), a faint green screen-glow from the side the device would be on (named per pose). No warm key light.
- **Background: a flat, uniform, pure magenta `#FF00FF`, nothing else**, so we can cut him out. No floor, no shadow
  on the background, no glow bleeding into it, no magenta on the character. Full body unless the pose says otherwise,
  with space around him; never cropped at the frame edge.
- 2048×2048 each.

## Rules

No text, letters, numerals, logos or brand marks anywhere (the pin and the mug are blank). No other characters, no
props except the mug in f05. Not pixel art, not a 2D cartoon, not a sticker with an outline. Inspect every image and
redo any that has text, a cropped limb, magenta on the frog, a non-uniform background, or a face that does not match
the others. The README lists each file, its size, and any rule it still misses.
